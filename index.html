    About Us

    Rooted in the growing belt, built for global trade.

    JIEX International Trade is a Maharashtra-based import-export company specializing in premium, export-grade fresh onions, connecting India's finest onion-growing regions with global markets. Headquartered in Ambajogai, Beed, we combine deep-rooted agricultural expertise with a commitment to quality, consistency, and reliable global logistics. Our mission is simple: to deliver farm-fresh, export-grade onions to international buyers with uncompromising quality standards, transparent processes, and timely fulfillment. From careful sourcing and grading to secure packaging and export documentation, every step of our process is built around trust and long-term partnerships. Backed by strong relationships with local farmers, JIEX International Trade is committed to promoting Indian agricultural exports on the world stage — one shipment, one partnership at a time.
