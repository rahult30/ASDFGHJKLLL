    How We Export

    From procurement to port, in six steps.

    01

    Sourcing

    Direct procurement from grower clusters, selected for size consistency and skin quality.

    02

    Grading & Sorting

    Machine grading by size, manual quality check to remove damaged or sprouted bulbs.

    03

    Packing

    Packed to buyer specification — mesh bags, cartons, or jumbo bags with custom labelling.

    04

    Pre-Shipment Inspection

    Third-party or buyer-nominated inspection available on request before container loading.

    05

    Documentation

    Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, and phytosanitary certificate prepared per shipment.

    06

    Shipping & Tracking

    FCL shipments via [Mundra / Nhava Sheva], with tracking details shared through to delivery.
